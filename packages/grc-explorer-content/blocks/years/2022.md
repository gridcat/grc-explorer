## MRC — claiming research rewards without staking

The 5.4.0.0 mandatory release, codenamed **Kermit's Mom**, shipped on **August 1, 2022** and introduced **Manual Reward Claims (MRC)** — the long-awaited mechanism for solo researchers to realise accumulated magnitude without having to stake actively.

The problem MRC solved: a researcher running BOINC on a single machine, not running a wallet 24/7 actively staking, would accumulate research-magnitude eligibility but rarely cash it in. Their reward potential just sat there. After Kermit's Mom, any researcher could compose a **manual claim transaction**, pay a fee to whatever staker mined it into a block, and convert their accumulated magnitude into actual on-chain GRC.

Effects:

- **Solo crunchers became first-class participants.** The reward system no longer privileged wallets that happened to also be staking nodes.
- **Stakers gained transaction fees** as compensation for including MRC claims, creating a small but real economic flow between the two participant classes.
- **Researcher activity decoupled from wallet uptime.** A laptop running BOINC overnight could now turn into GRC even without leaving a wallet running.

## What else Kermit's Mom shipped

Beyond MRC, the release rolled in:

- **Constant Block Rewards (CBR) refinements** to the post-[Fern](/blocks/2020) reward calculation — tuning the constant subsidy and integrating it cleanly with the new MRC fee flow.
- **Codebase modernisation** continuing the pattern Fern set — Bitcoin upstream alignment, dependency updates, build improvements.
- **Wallet UX polish** around the new MRC operations.

## Why "Kermit's Mom"?

Gridcoin's mandatory release codename tradition: the **Miss Piggy** milestone covered the 5.4.x family of releases, and the major feature releases got named after Muppets characters in roughly the order they shipped. Kermit's Mom was the first mandatory in the series — the next mandatory's codename would carry the milestone forward. Pure community whimsy, but unmistakable in changelogs.
