## A year off from forks

[Kermit's Mom (2022)](/blocks/2022) was a busy mandatory release. 2023 was the year all of it had to just work, and the consensus log stays empty: **{{stat:total_blocks}} blocks**, **{{stat:total_superblocks}} superblocks**, **{{stat:total_txs}} transactions**, **{{stat:grc_moved}} GRC** moved, **{{stat:grc_minted}} GRC** minted, protocol identical to the year before.

The releases stayed in leisure territory, nothing mandatory. The one worth naming is **5.4.2.0 "LaVerne"** on **March 26, 2023**, which finally brought a hierarchical-deterministic (BIP32) wallet ([^5.4.2.0 release notes — github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases/tag/5.4.2.0)). HD wallets had been a standing community wish for years, so this is the kind of release that does not change consensus but does change daily life for anyone managing keys. A run of small fixes followed through April, up to **5.4.5.0** on the 23rd ([^Gridcoin Research releases — github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases)).

## What a full year of MRC actually looked like

[Manual Reward Claims](/blocks/2022) had its first complete year, and a few patterns settled in. Solo researchers actually used it, so claim transactions became a regular if low-volume sight rather than a theoretical feature. Stakers earned a steady trickle of MRC fees for including them. And magnitude eligibility stopped being tied to wallet uptime, because a claim could be filed days or weeks after the work was done. None of that is newsworthy the way a hard fork is. It is the quieter and harder thing: a design working at scale instead of just in the issue that proposed it.

## What was being assembled

The next big upgrade was taking shape in design discussions through 2023, though the part people often misremember is the timing. The community framework for a treasury and protocol-enforced sidestaking was not finalised by a 2023 poll. It is tracked in the project's task repo as a poll opened in January 2024 ([^Treasury and Mandatory Sidestaking poll — Gridcoin-Tasks #263, github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Tasks/issues/263)). 2023 is the year the idea was argued; the formal vote and the code came after.

The shape being discussed: a protocol-enforced cap (the consensus code lands on 25%) on a sidestake redirected from staking rewards, up to four mandatory sidestake outputs on a coinstake, with recipients set through an administrative contract so allocations can change without another hard fork. All of that needed a new block version to carry it. That work is the spine of the [2026 Natasha release](/blocks/2026), and as this archive's data shows, it has not activated on mainnet yet.
