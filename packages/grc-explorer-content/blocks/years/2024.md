## Still v12, but moving

2024 sat between two protocol regimes. Mainnet kept running under the [v12 / Kermit's Mom](/blocks/2022) rules, so the on-chain numbers look like another steady year: **{{stat:total_blocks}} blocks**, **{{stat:total_superblocks}} superblocks**, **{{stat:total_txs}} transactions**, **{{stat:grc_moved}} GRC** moved, **{{stat:grc_minted}} GRC** minted. Consensus behaviour was identical to 2023. The motion was everywhere except the protocol.

In January the **Treasury and Mandatory Sidestaking** poll opened in the project's task repo ([^Treasury and Mandatory Sidestaking poll — Gridcoin-Tasks #263, github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Tasks/issues/263)). This is the decision that decides how Gridcoin funds itself: a protocol-enforced sidestake out of staking rewards, hard-capped in consensus at 25%, up to four mandatory outputs on a coinstake, recipients changeable through an administrative contract so the allocation can move without a future hard fork. The community putting its own funding model to an on-chain vote, rather than a maintainer deciding it, is the most Gridcoin thing in this archive.

## The release line

**5.4.6.0 "Miss Piggy"** on **March 2, 2024** is the structurally important one ([^5.4.6.0 release notes — github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases/tag/5.4.6.0)). It introduced the CMake build system alongside the old autotools, shipped the sidestake GUI and registry plus a `listmandatorysidestakes` RPC, and otherwise stayed a leisure release: the code for what was coming sat in the wallet, dormant, not yet consensus. A hotfix followed two days later, then **5.4.7.0** on March 13, which started refusing connections from nodes older than 5.4.5.0 (a network-hygiene move ahead of the bigger upgrade), and **5.4.8.0** on April 11 ([^Gridcoin Research releases — github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases)).

After this the active branch turned almost entirely toward the next mandatory release and its testnet validation, which is the [2025](/blocks/2025) story.

## What 2024 was, honestly

A bridging year is unglamorous and easy to skip in a history. It is also where a careful project does the boring half of a hard fork: get the build system modern, put the dormant code in the wallet, ban the stragglers, and let the community vote before anyone touches consensus. None of v13 or v14 activated on mainnet in 2024, and as this archive's live data shows, none of it has activated on mainnet yet.
