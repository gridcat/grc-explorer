## Before Research, there was Classic

The chain this explorer indexes — Gridcoin **Research** — starts at [block 0 in October 2014](/block/0). But that wasn't the first Gridcoin. A year earlier, on **October 16, 2013**, Rob Hälförd (a pseudonym) launched **Gridcoin Classic**: the original chain, with the original idea. Classic is a different chain on a different codebase, with its own ticker and its own block history; the explorer here doesn't track it. This page exists so the chronicle is complete.

## What Classic actually did

Classic ran a **hybrid Proof-of-Work** scheme. Each computer on the network split its time: half spent hashing (securing the chain, Bitcoin-style), half spent contributing to BOINC science projects (the value-add). The pitch was straightforward — better than Bitcoin, where 100% of the compute is wasted. But the design still left half the network's electricity going into hash functions whose only product was more hash functions.

That trade-off was Classic's central tension. Hybrid-PoW was *progress* over pure Bitcoin, but it wasn't yet the answer. The community spent the next year asking what a chain would look like that put **all** of its compute into useful work, and used staking — not hashing — for security.

## The transition

The answer was Gridcoin Research: a fresh chain, not a hard fork. Classic kept running alongside it during a deliberate end-of-life window:

- **2014-10-12** — conversion window opens. Holders begin migrating Classic balances to Research at a **1 Classic → 10 Research** ratio, by burning to a designated address with no recoverable private key.
- **2015-04-26** — conversion window closes after roughly six months. Unconverted Classic balances + accrued interest are routed into a community foundation fund.

During that window the two chains traded under different tickers: Classic became **GRCX**, Research kept the original **GRC** ticker. Confirmation requirements scaled with conversion size — 10 confirmations for ≤100 coins, up to 300 confirmations above 10,000 — to keep the burn-and-mint exchange honest.

Classic itself ran in slow-decay mode through that window: rewards cut to a fixed 10 coins per block for the first five months, then 0.1 coins per block thereafter, signalling the chain's planned wind-down.

## Why this matters for the chronicle

If you came here expecting on-chain data for 2013, you'll find the data section below empty — Classic isn't indexed by this explorer. But the *idea* of compute-for-science, the BOINC integration playbook, the founder, and the early community all came from Classic. [The Gridcoin Research chain](/blocks/2014) inherited the goal and rebuilt the technology to deliver on it without the hybrid-PoW compromise.
