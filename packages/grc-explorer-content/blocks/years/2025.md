## A mainnet year with no protocol change

Mainnet ran the [v12 rules](/blocks/2022) it had used since 2022: **{{stat:total_blocks}} blocks**, **{{stat:total_superblocks}} superblocks**, **{{stat:total_txs}} transactions**, **{{stat:grc_moved}} GRC** moved, **{{stat:grc_minted}} GRC** minted. The protocol didn't move. The decisions and the testing for the next fork happened this year.

## The reward vote

The two reward polls that opened in [December 2024](/blocks/2024) closed on January 26. The rebalancing proposal passed with a large majority. In the companion poll on how much to raise staking and research rewards, "500%" came first with about 42% of the weight, ahead of six other options ([^Multi-choice poll outcomes, Gridcoin-Tasks #270](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Tasks/issues/270)).

That left a question nobody had settled: how do you read a multiple-choice poll with numeric answers? Taking the winner gives 500%. Averaging the votes by weight gives about 979%. A third poll, open from March 5 to April 16, asked exactly that, and first-past-the-post won by a wide margin. So 500% it was. The code to make reward levels adjustable without a hard fork went into the testnet builds in April. The increase itself reached mainnet with the [2026 fork](/blocks/2026).

Other polls were lighter. A proposal to rename the halford, Gridcoin's smallest unit, failed. An October poll titled "Alive, are we?" drew a large turnout and a near-unanimous yes.

## What testnet ran

5.4.9.0 on February 16 was a leisure release for mainnet, described in its notes as probably the last before the Natasha mandatory ([^5.4.9.0 release notes, github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases/tag/5.4.9.0)). It added macOS builds for Apple Silicon, fixes for newer compilers and Boost, and a datadir fix for Flatpak installs.

The real work went out as testnet pre-releases ([^Gridcoin Research releases, github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases)):

- **5.4.9.3-testnet** on April 22 was mandatory for testnet and set the **testnet** v13 height at block 2,870,000. It switched on mandatory sidestaking, Superblock v3, AutoGreylist, the adjustable magnitude unit and magnitude-weight factor, and a new 500 GRC ceiling for the block reward.
- **5.4.9.5-testnet** on June 19 added optional Qt 6, poll notifications, and a fix for a memory bug in AutoGreylist. It also renamed "investors" to "non-crunchers" in the code. (A poll in 2022 had voted against that rename. The code changed anyway.)
- **5.4.9.7-testnet** on December 8 was mostly build work: Qt 6 dependencies, Boost 1.89 and GCC 15.

A note on numbers, because this is where a common mix-up starts. 2,870,000 is a testnet height. So is 3,126,500, the testnet v14 height, which wasn't set until March 2026. Neither is where the upgrade happened on the chain this explorer indexes. Mainnet's heights were 3,989,800 and 3,990,000. HTLC support, v3 beacons, the CMake-only build, Docker images and Flatpak packaging all landed in early 2026, not in 2025.

## The whitelist and the market

World Community Grid came back to the whitelist on January 22, and Minecraft@home three days later. SiDock@home, TN-Grid and GPUGrid moved on and off the greylist during the year. At year end twelve projects were active. A proposal to re-whitelist Denis@Home stalled because the project had no work to hand out.

A long thread in the task repo weighed a listing on a major exchange. The obstacles named there were the lack of a legal opinion on whether GRC is a security, no wallet audit and very little volume. GRC stayed on SafeTrade and FreiExchange. On December 31 it traded at about $0.007, for a market cap around $3.5 million.

The milestone after Natasha opened in May. Its name, keeping to the alphabet, is "Opossum."
