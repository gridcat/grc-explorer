## The year of the rehearsal

Mainnet did not change in 2025. It ran the same [v12 / MRC-era](/blocks/2022) rules it had since 2022: **{{stat:total_blocks}} mainnet blocks**, **{{stat:total_superblocks}} superblocks**, **{{stat:total_txs}} transactions**, **{{stat:grc_moved}} GRC** moved, **{{stat:grc_minted}} GRC** minted. Everything interesting in 2025 happened on testnet.

This is the year to be careful about numbers, because it is where a common mistake comes from. Testnet activated v13 at testnet block 2,870,000 and v14 at testnet block 3,126,500. Those are **testnet** heights. They are not where the upgrade happens on the chain this explorer indexes. Mainnet's v13 and v14 heights are different and much higher, and as the [2026 page](/blocks/2026) shows with live data, mainnet had not reached them at all. If you have seen "the Gridcoin upgrade activated at block 3,126,500," that is the testnet rehearsal being mistaken for the real thing.

## What testnet was exercising

The **5.4.9.x** line shipped as a series of testnet pre-releases through the year, each validating a slice of what the eventual mainnet release would carry ([^Gridcoin Research releases — github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases)):

- **Mandatory sidestaking** under SuperblockV3 contracts, the protocol-enforced sidestake with the 25% consensus cap and an administrative contract for recipients.
- **v3 beacons**, which require a cryptographic proof that you control the BOINC account, not just the username-change ritual Fern introduced. It closes the last spoof class the [2017 security work](/blocks/2017) did not.
- **HTLC** support, the BIP65/BIP68/BIP112 primitives (`OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY`, `OP_CHECKSEQUENCEVERIFY`) that make trustless cross-chain atomic swaps possible. Worth being precise here: HTLC is the on-chain building block, not a swap product. An atomic-swap protocol on top is separate work, not something the release itself ships.

The pre-release series doubled as a build-system cleanup that actually reached users: CMake became the only supported build, Qt 6 landed alongside Qt 5, and packaging grew Flatpak, official Docker images, and Alpine/musl support. **5.4.9.0** in February ([^5.4.9.0 release notes — github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases/tag/5.4.9.0)) carried ARM64 macOS and Flatpak; the testnet builds (5.4.9.3 in April, 5.4.9.7 in December, and the iterations around them) hardened the consensus changes.

## What 2025 set up

Every piece of the upcoming mainnet upgrade got months of testnet mileage in 2025. That is the entire point of the year: by the time the mainnet release shipped in [2026](/blocks/2026), nothing in it would be untested. The mainnet switch itself was deliberately left for later, behind a block height the chain had not reached.
