## A bridging year

2024 sat between two protocol regimes: still operating under the [Kermit's Mom (2022)](/blocks/2022) MRC era on mainnet, but with serious testnet activity laying the groundwork for the v13/v14 mandatory upgrades that would activate in [2026](/blocks/2026). On mainnet itself, {{stat:total_blocks}} blocks, {{stat:total_superblocks}} superblocks, {{stat:total_txs}} transactions, {{stat:grc_moved}} GRC moved — protocol behaviour identical to 2023.

The headline mainnet release was **5.4.6.0** (March 2, 2024) — the *final* leisure release in the **Miss Piggy** milestone. Significance:

- **Wallet stability** fixes addressing accumulated edge cases.
- **Infrastructure for the v13 block-version protocol** was added — code paths sat in the wallet ready to activate, but didn't change consensus until a later mandatory release.

After 5.4.6.0, the active development branch turned almost entirely to the next mandatory release.

## v14 and the path to mandatory sidestaking

The Treasury and Mandatory Sidestaking poll that had passed in 2023 framed the goal: a protocol-enforced sidestake (capped at 25% of any coinstake reward) routed to the Gridcoin foundation, with up to four mandatory sidestake outputs allowed on a coinstake transaction. Implementation required a new block version (**v14**) to carry the new consensus rules.

The 2024 testnet work focused on validating that v14 hard-fork mechanics:

- **Mandatory sidestake validation** in the block-reward claim checker.
- **`MaxMandatorySideStakeTotalAlloc`** as a consensus parameter.
- **Administrative contract** machinery to update sidestake recipient addresses without further hard forks.

In parallel, the **v3 beacon** design — replacing the post-Fern v2 beacons with an authentication scheme requiring cryptographic ownership proof — moved from spec into prototype. v3 beacons would close a remaining identity-spoof vector (a researcher proving they own a BOINC CPID, beyond the username-change ritual that had been sufficient since Fern).

Plus **HTLC** (Hash Time-Locked Contracts) — the on-chain primitive used in atomic swaps — went into the protocol queue. Its presence wouldn't unlock atomic swaps automatically, but it was the building block.

## Wallet line

5.4.6.0 was the only mainnet leisure release of the year. Several testnet pre-releases (5.4.7.0+, 5.4.9.x) shipped through late 2024 and into 2025 to validate v14 mechanics on testnet at activation height **3,126,500**. None of that changed mainnet consensus during 2024.
