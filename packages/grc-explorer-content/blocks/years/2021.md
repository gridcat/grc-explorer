## The year Fern had to actually run

[Fern (2020)](/blocks/2020) was the biggest change the chain had ever taken. 2021 was the year it had to survive contact with reality for twelve straight months, and it did. **{{stat:total_blocks}} blocks**, **{{stat:total_superblocks}} superblocks**, **{{stat:total_txs}} transactions**, **{{stat:grc_moved}} GRC** moved, all under the new beacon lifecycle, the superblock-windows reward calculation, and a network with no team requirement.

The punchline is that there is no punchline. Beacons expired at 180 days and got renewed without anyone coordinating it ([^Fern beacon lifecycle — 5.0.0.0 release notes, github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases/tag/5.0.0.0)). The reward math paid out cleanly. Removing the team requirement did not break attribution. By the end of the year the post-Fern protocol was not "the new way Gridcoin works." It was just how Gridcoin works.

The one mandatory event was **5.2.0.0 "Hilda"** on **March 1, 2021**, with its mandatory cutover around [block 2,197,000](/block/2197000) ([^5.2.0.0 release notes — github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases/tag/5.2.0.0)). It was a required upgrade rather than a block-version break like Fern: stability and protocol housekeeping on top of the v11 base, not a new consensus era.

## What was being built underneath

Two threads ran through 2021's design discussions and neither shipped this year:

**Manual Reward Claims.** Solo crunchers, the people running BOINC without keeping a wallet online to stake, accumulated magnitude they could not easily turn into GRC. The fix being designed: let any wallet file a claim transaction for its own accrued research reward and pay the including staker a fee for the trouble. That landed in [2022](/blocks/2022).

**Funding the project from the protocol.** The standing question of how Gridcoin pays for its own development without passing a hat. The mechanics that eventually became mandatory sidestaking started here as conversation. They are still not live on mainnet as of [2026](/blocks/2026), which tells you how carefully this community moves money questions.

2021 is a maintenance year, and on a volunteer chain a clean maintenance year right after the largest rewrite in its history is the system passing its hardest test: being left alone and not falling over.
