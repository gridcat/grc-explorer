## A boring year — and that's the win

[Fern (2020)](/blocks/2020) was the largest single architectural change in the chain's history. 2021 was the year it had to actually run, every day, without surprises. {{stat:total_blocks}} blocks, {{stat:total_superblocks}} superblocks, {{stat:total_txs}} transactions, {{stat:grc_moved}} GRC moved — all under the new beacon-verification, superblock-windows, no-team-requirement regime.

The fact that the chain operated routinely is the year's punchline. The new beacon lifecycle worked. Renewal at 180 days happened without coordination. The superblock-windows reward calculation paid out cleanly. The team-requirement removal didn't break magnitude attribution. By year-end, the post-Fern protocol was simply *how Gridcoin works.*

## What was being built underneath

Two threads ran in parallel through 2021's design discussions:

- **Manual Reward Claims (MRC).** Solo researchers — the ones running BOINC without staking actively — accumulated magnitude they couldn't easily realise as GRC because they weren't staking. The proposal: let any wallet *manually claim* their accumulated research reward by sending a transaction that included the claim, paying the staker a fee for inclusion. This would land in [2022's Kermit's Mom release](/blocks/2022).
- **Foundation/treasury sidestaking.** A standing question about how to fund Gridcoin's own development and infrastructure costs from the network rather than from voluntary donations. The mechanics would mature into 2026's mandatory sidestaking.

Neither shipped in 2021 — both were on the design table.

## Wallet line

The 5.0.x line continued with leisure (non-mandatory) releases through the year, addressing wallet stability, RPC improvements, and the kind of small fixes that accumulate after a major architectural cut.
