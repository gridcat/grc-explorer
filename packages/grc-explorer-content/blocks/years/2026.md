## Released is not the same as activated

This is the page most likely to be read while it is still wrong somewhere else, so let me be exact about what happened and what has not.

On **April 3, 2026**, the mandatory **5.5.0.0** release, codenamed **"Natasha,"** shipped ([^5.5.0.0 release notes — github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases/tag/5.5.0.0)). That is the software release date. It is not a fork. Natasha's v13 and v14 consensus rules activate on mainnet at **block 3,989,800** and **block 3,990,000**, roughly eight weeks after release, and nodes have to upgrade before the chain gets there.

As of the data this explorer has indexed, the chain has **not** gotten there. Check the [history index](/history): mainnet is still minting under the [v12 / Kermit's Mom](/blocks/2022) rules it has run since 2022. So far in 2026 that means **{{stat:total_blocks}} blocks**, **{{stat:total_superblocks}} superblocks**, **{{stat:total_txs}} transactions**, **{{stat:grc_moved}} GRC** moved, **{{stat:grc_minted}} GRC** minted, and counting, with the indexer cursor still short of the v13 height. Everything Natasha changes is real, tested, and scheduled. None of it is live on the chain you are looking at.

If you have read elsewhere that "Natasha activated April 3" or "the fork happened at block 3,126,500," that is the [testnet rehearsal](/blocks/2025) being reported as mainnet. Testnet activated v14 at testnet block 3,126,500. Mainnet's number is 3,990,000, and the difference is the whole point of this page.

## What Natasha carries

It is a large release: roughly 78 pull requests and 435 commits over about thirteen months, on the order of fifty thousand lines added ([^5.5.0.0 release notes — github.com/gridcoin-community](https://github.com/gridcoin-community/Gridcoin-Research/releases/tag/5.5.0.0)). The community nickname for the v13 half is "Fern II," a wink back to the [2020 rewrite](/blocks/2020), and it is earned.

**v13** brings mandatory sidestaking under SuperblockV3 contracts, the AutoGreylist system for BOINC projects, a changeable magnitude unit and magnitude-weight factor set through protocol entries, ProjectV4 data, the v3 poll format, and poll notifications. This is where the [treasury and mandatory-sidestaking vote](/blocks/2024) finally becomes consensus: a sidestake out of staking rewards, capped at 25% in the protocol, up to four outputs, recipients changeable through an administrative contract.

**v14** is the HTLC fork. It ports BIP65, BIP68 and BIP112 (`OP_CHECKLOCKTIMEVERIFY`, `OP_CHECKSEQUENCEVERIFY`, sequence-locked inputs), with `createhtlc` / `claimhtlc` / `refundhtlc` RPCs, and **v3 beacons** that require an RSA-SHA512 ownership proof from the BOINC project server. That closes the last identity-spoof class that survived [Fern's](/blocks/2020) username-change verification.

The build story from [2025's testnet line](/blocks/2025) is now the only story: CMake is the sole supported build, Qt 6 alongside Qt 5, with Flatpak, Docker and Alpine packaging.

## On atomic swaps, precisely

v14 ships the HTLC primitives that make trustless cross-chain atomic swaps possible. That is the accurate claim and the limit of it. HTLC is a building block. An actual Gridcoin atomic-swap protocol built on top is separate work and is not part of this release. Anyone telling you Natasha "adds atomic swaps" is skipping a step the code does not skip.

## Where this archive stands

This page is being read during a year still in progress, before its defining fork has activated on mainnet. That is an unusual thing for a history article to admit, and it is the honest state of the chain. When the indexer cursor crosses block 3,990,000, the numbers above keep climbing and the protocol underneath them changes. Until then, Natasha is the release that is ready and the fork that is scheduled, and this explorer will show you the exact block it becomes real.
