## Fern — the architectural inflection point

If [4.0.0.0 (2018)](/blocks/2018) changed Gridcoin's economics, **Fern** changed almost everything else. Released as 5.0.0.0-Mandatory on **September 3, 2020**, with hard-fork activation at **block 2,053,000** around October 4, Fern was — in the announcement's own words — "essentially a complete rewrite," touching **240 pull requests** of merged code while staying compatible with the v10 protocol the prior 4.x line had run.

This is the upgrade most often called *the* defining change in Gridcoin's modern era. The headline features:

### Superblock Windows reward calculation

Research rewards used to be averaged over a rolling window — convenient, but it imposed awkward constraints (the 500 GRC first-stake limit, the six-month reward expiration). Fern switched to direct calculation **from magnitudes in each superblock between stakes**. A researcher's reward at stake-time depends on every superblock between their previous stake and this one, with no averaging artifact in the way. Both old constraints were removed.

### Beacon system overhaul

Beacons got a real lifecycle and real verification:

- One-time verification on registration via a BOINC project username change — proves the wallet owner controls the BOINC account.
- Advertisement fee raised from 0.00001 GRC to 0.5 GRC — small but enough to deter spam beacons.
- Beacons expire after 180 days and require renewal. Critically, **earned research rewards are retained** even if the beacon lapses; only future earning is paused.

### Team requirement removed

Researchers no longer had to join a specific Gridcoin BOINC team to earn rewards. This had been a sticky onboarding step for years; removing it lowered the barrier for new participants without affecting magnitude attribution.

### Sync improvements

Whole-mainnet sync went from "snapshot-dependent" to **under four hours from genesis** — a meaningful UX improvement for anyone bringing up a new node.

### Contract and voting redesign

Polls, beacons, and other contract-bearing transactions got a modernised handling pipeline alongside refined fee structures.

Everything that came after Fern (MRC, sidestaking, v13, v14) layers on top. Fern itself stays the structural reference point.
