## The testnet year

2025 was when v14 stopped being design work and became *running code on testnet*. The 5.4.9.x line shipped multiple testnet pre-releases through the year, each one validating a slice of what the eventual mainnet upgrade — [Natasha (5.5.0.0)](/blocks/2026) — would carry. On mainnet, the chain stayed in the same MRC-era protocol shape that had run since 2022; {{stat:total_blocks}} mainnet blocks were minted, {{stat:total_superblocks}} superblocks issued, {{stat:total_txs}} transactions processed, {{stat:grc_moved}} GRC moved.

## The v14 rehearsal

Testnet activated v14 at height **3,126,500** so the validators could exercise:

- **Mandatory sidestaking** — protocol-enforced sidestake outputs (up to four per coinstake), capped at 25% total reward allocation, validated by the block-reward claim checker. Recipient addresses settable through an administrative contract.
- **v3 beacons** — replacing post-Fern v2 beacons with cryptographic ownership proof. Researchers prove they control the CPID rather than just the BOINC username, closing a remaining spoof class.
- **HTLC support** (Hash Time-Locked Contracts) — Bitcoin BIP65/BIP112 semantics ported into Gridcoin. This is the primitive atomic swaps need; its presence opens the design space for cross-chain swap protocols (already publicly in development by core Gridcoin contributors during 2025-2026).

Major testnet pre-releases:

- **5.4.9.5-testnet** (June 19, 2025) — added poll notifications, Qt 6 support, and beacon-interval adjustments.
- **5.4.9.7-testnet** (December 8, 2025) — GCC 15 compatibility, Boost 1.89+ support, Alpine Linux CI.
- **5.4.9.9-testnet** (February 15, 2026) and **5.4.9.11-testnet** (March 5, 2026) — final hardening passes addressing fractional scaling, build-system improvements, and autogreylisting refinements.

The testnet pre-release series doubled as a build-system modernisation: **CMake replaced the legacy autotools build**, **Qt 6 compatibility** landed for the GUI wallet, and the CI matrix expanded to cover modern compilers and a wider Linux distro set.

## What 2025 set up

Every piece of the Natasha hard fork that activated April 3, 2026 — sidestaking, v3 beacons, HTLC — got its rehearsal in 2025. The mainnet cutover was a ceremony of confirming what testnet had already been demonstrating for months.
